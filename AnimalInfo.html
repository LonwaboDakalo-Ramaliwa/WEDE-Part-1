Dogs

Dogs are known for their loyalty, companionship, and playful nature. They thrive on regular exercise, proper training, and social interaction.

Quick Facts

  • Average lifespan: 10–15 years
  • Diet: Balanced dog food (kibble, wet food, or raw diet if supervised)
  • Exercise: Daily walks and playtime
  • Temperament: Loyal, social, and affectionate

Cats

Cats are independent animals who can also be very affectionate. They enjoy exploring, playing, and finding cozy places to nap.

Quick Facts

  • Average lifespan: 12–18 years
  • Diet: High-protein cat food (wet or dry)
  • Exercise: Indoor play or safe outdoor exploration
  • Temperament: Independent, curious, and loving

Other Pets

Small pets such as rabbits, guinea pigs, and hamsters are gentle companions, especially for families seeking lower-maintenance animals.

Quick Facts

  • Average lifespan: 2–10 years (depending on species)
  • Diet: Fresh vegetables, pellets, hay, or species-specific food
  • Exercise: Playtime in secure enclosures
  • Temperament: Gentle, calm, and best with patient handling

Frequently Asked Questions

General Animal Questions

How do I know which pet is right for me?

Consider your lifestyle, living space, time commitment, and budget. Dogs often need more time and exercise, while cats and small pets can adapt better to limited spaces.

Are shelter animals healthy?

Yes, animals from our shelter are checked by veterinarians, vaccinated, and treated before adoption.

Specific Animal Questions

How often should I walk my dog?

Most dogs need at least two walks per day, plus playtime to keep them mentally and physically stimulated.

Should I keep my cat indoors?

Indoor cats tend to live longer and healthier lives. If allowed outside, it’s best to supervise or provide a secure outdoor enclosure.

What do rabbits need in their diet?

Rabbits need unlimited hay, fresh vegetables, and small amounts of pellets. Fresh water should always be available.

Donations and Volunteering

How can I donate?

You can donate money, supplies, or food. Visit our Donations & Volunteering page for details.

Can I volunteer at the shelter?

Yes! We welcome volunteers for walking dogs, socializing cats, cleaning, and helping with events. Sign up on our volunteer page.

Are donations tax-deductible?

Yes, as a registered non-profit, your donations are tax-deductible. We provide receipts for all contributions.
